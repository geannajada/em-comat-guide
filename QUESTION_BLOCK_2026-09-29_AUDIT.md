### Corrections that matter for boards

| Note shorthand | Final interpretation | Confidence / inspected evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Panic attacks = panic disorder | Recurrent unexpected attacks plus at least one month of worry/behavior change; distinguish attack from disorder. | Moderate · First Aid PDF p. 609 |
| Meningioma → surgery then chemotherapy | Observation in selected asymptomatic cases; surgery when indicated, selected radiotherapy. Chemotherapy is not routine. | Moderate · First Aid PDF pp. 416–417; EANO treatment recommendations linked in the guide |
| Thiazide → lactic acidosis | Hypokalemic metabolic alkalosis, with hyponatremia and the hyperGLUC pattern. | Moderate · First Aid PDF p. 73 |
| Risperidone = high-potency typical antipsychotic | Risperidone is atypical; haloperidol is high-potency typical. Both can cause akathisia. | Moderate · First Aid PDF pp. 605–606 |
| DDAVP excess = SIADH | Exogenous antidiuresis can cause water retention and hyponatremia; this is not increased endogenous ADH secretion. | High · DailyMed desmopressin label linked in the guide |
| Lithium toxicity always → dialysis | Severity, renal function, level and expected clearance determine dialysis. Severe neurologic/cardiac toxicity is urgent. | High · EXTRIP lithium criteria; Moderate · First Aid PDF p. 619 |
| Foodborne botulism requires germination in vivo | Foodborne disease usually involves preformed toxin; infant/wound disease involves in-vivo toxin production. | Moderate · First Aid PDF pp. 399–400 |
| Every acute prostatitis case needs six weeks | Culture-directed therapy for weeks; First Aid gives 4–6 weeks for mild acute disease. Duration depends on clinical context and response. | Moderate · First Aid PDF p. 723; not presented as a universal regimen |
| Smoking is always the strongest rupture predictor | Preserve First Aid's smoking clue while explaining Harrison's diameter, growth and symptom-based risk assessment. | Moderate · First Aid PDF p. 88 versus Harrison PDF pp. 2210–2211 |
| Mixing study is the initial test / DDAVP treats all hemophilia | Start with coagulation screening; mixing studies distinguish deficiency from inhibitor patterns; factor assays confirm. DDAVP is for selected responsive mild A, not B. | Moderate · First Aid PDF p. 288; High · DailyMed desmopressin label |
| Wedge-shaped lung infarct is pathognomonic for PE | Suggestive infarction clue; direct CTPA evidence is a pulmonary-artery filling defect. | Moderate · Harrison PDF pp. 2203–2204 |
| Fibrinolysis is not indicated for ACS/STEMI | No fibrinolysis for UA/NSTEMI. Eligible STEMI with excessive PCI delay may receive it, then transfer. Existing correct pathway retained. | Moderate · Existing ACS pathway, supplied First Aid PDF pp. 59–68 and linked ACS guidance |
| Hemorrhoids can never cause instability | Usually small-volume bleeding; do not dismiss significant bleeding as hemorrhoidal without evaluation. | Moderate · Existing GI bleeding pathway and supplied textbook context |
| IO tibia because it is distant from sternum | Accessible anteromedial tibial surface; humeral and device-specific sternal alternatives. Selection depends on age, device and injuries. | High · Defence Medical Command IO guidance; Moderate · Harrison PDF p. 2379 |
