**The guide now contains 152 numbered study-reference blocks and 39 next-step pathways, with all 13 NBOME presentation categories represented.** The existing tabs, search, emphasis style, collapsible references and two attributed visual references were preserved.

**Limit:** this is an extensive topic-level audit with focused claim verification, not a line-by-line certification of every sentence against every page of all three textbooks. ## Confidence and emphasis

- **High:** the specific linked recommendation is directly supported by an official guideline or statement.
- **Moderate:** textbook or specialty educational synthesis, including clinically conditional recommendations.
- **Low:** limited evidence.
- **Unconfirmed:** not independently verified, including NBOME adoption of recent changes and claims of exact condition-level exam frequency.

These are editorial confidence categories, not formal GRADE ratings. Bold identifies the answer/action, underlining identifies the distinguishing clue, and bold red identifies a dangerous delay, contraindication or critical branch. Yield badges express study priority rather than measured question frequency.

### Where newer guidance or clinical qualifications matter

- **High:** the 2024 hyperglycemic-crisis consensus uses a potassium threshold that differs from older DKA teaching. The guide labels the current threshold instead of mixing versions. [2024 consensus](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00125-024-06183-8)
- **High:** the FDA states that U.S. Andexxa commercial sales ended December 22, 2025 and describes adverse safety findings. Older antidote associations need that qualification. [FDA notice](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/update-safety-andexxa)
- **High:** ABA 2024 guidance recommends an initial 2 mL/kg/%TBSA approach for adults with burns ≥20% TBSA; traditional Parkland 4 mL teaching is retained only as a named formula, not a universal fixed-volume order. Fluids require titration. [ABA guideline](https://guidance.nattrauma.org/media/wetjtmrg/american-burn-association-clinical-practice-guidelines-on-burn-shock-resuscitation.pdf)
- **High:** outpatient PID guidance includes metronidazole with ceftriaxone/doxycycline. [CDC PID](https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/pid.htm)
- **High:** anaphylaxis guidance prioritizes epinephrine and does not recommend routine corticosteroid emergency treatment. [Resuscitation Council UK](https://www.resus.org.uk/library/additional-guidance/guidance-anaphylaxis)
- **High:** selected gout patients may start indicated urate-lowering therapy during a flare; the old blanket prohibition was removed. [ACR guideline](https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltee37abb6b278ab2c/blt04d52e3b6ff5112f/632cab5b258fb55f6b2186af/gout-guideline-2020.pdf)
- **High:** adult severe foreign-body obstruction has an updated 2025 sequence. [AHA adult BLS](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/adult-basic-life-support)
- **High:** tetanus immune globulin has additional dirty-wound indications with HIV/severe immunodeficiency. [CDC wound guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/tetanus/hcp/clinical-guidance/index.html)
- **High:** cecal and sigmoid volvulus should not share one detorsion pathway. [ASCRS guideline](https://www.ascrsu.com/ascrs/repview?name=2_2851057_PDF&type=784-166)
- **Moderate:** primary and secondary angle closure can need different treatment. [AAO EyeWiki](https://eyewiki.org/Primary_vs._Secondary_Angle_Closure_Glaucoma)
- **Moderate:** wound closure follows tissue assessment, contamination and host factors, not a universal clock cutoff. [SAEM wound management](https://www.saem.org/about-saem/academies-interest-groups-affiliates2/cdem/for-students/online-education/m3-curriculum/group-emergency-department-procedures/basic-wound-management)

Existing linked 2026 stroke/PE/postmenopausal-bleeding material remains in the guide. NBOME's timing of adoption of new guidance is **Unconfirmed**; this guide does not claim access to the item bank or future exam content.
