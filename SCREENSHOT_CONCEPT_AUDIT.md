## Corrections made to shorthand

- Aspiration pneumonia occurs in community and hospital settings; bacterial infection and chemical pneumonitis require different decisions.
- Balanitis care means gentle hygiene and avoiding irritants, with an antifungal when Candida is suspected. Do not forcibly retract the foreskin.
- Closed eyes or a normal routine EEG alone cannot establish functional seizures.
- Eclampsia can occur postpartum and does not require documented proteinuria.
- Stimulant seizures are not invariably hyponatremic; MDMA specifically raises concern for acute hyponatremia.
- Beta-blocker poisoning is not a universal six-hour observation or glucagon-only algorithm.
- Ruptured AAA requires aortic assessment; a negative FAST cannot exclude retroperitoneal rupture.
- Bronchiolitis typically affects children under two years, not under two days.
- A stone over 10 mm often needs intervention but does not automatically require hospital admission or lithotripsy.
- Intussusception does not require currant-jelly stool, and unstable patients require stabilization/surgical assessment before a rigid imaging sequence.
- Kawasaki disease is inflammatory vasculitis, not a contagious exanthem.
- Open-angle glaucoma can occur without an elevated measured IOP.

## Evidence and scope

High = directly supported by an inspected guideline or official clinical reference. Moderate = textbook synthesis, clinical pattern rather than a diagnostic rule, older consensus, or management that requires contextual interpretation. These labels describe support for this educational summary, not formal GRADE ratings or a guarantee of exam coverage. Study priority and selective emphasis are editorial judgments, not measured UWorld/NBOME frequency.

The supplied screenshots were treated as learning objectives, not as authoritative medical instructions. Relevant supplied textbook passages were inspected alongside official/professional guidance. This pass audits the screenshot concepts and overlapping guide entries; it does not claim every sentence of the entire guide was rechecked against every page of all three textbooks. Source links are beside the corresponding additions.
